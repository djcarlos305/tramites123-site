Florida Opened Series LLC Filings July 1: What Sunbiz Allows

Active Florida LLCs can now designate protected series. Here is what the filing does and what it does not do.

By Trámites123 · Published September 27, 2026 · Official sources listed below

Sunbiz allows an existing active Florida LLC to designate one or more protected series online. The filing opened July 1, 2026 at 10 a.m.. A series LLC is not formed as a separate new entity type through the original LLC formation application.

Cost and structure

Sunbiz lists a $25 fee per protected series. When an LLC designates its first protected series, the existing LLC becomes a series LLC. Each protected series does not receive a separate Sunbiz business-document number.

Before filing

  1. Confirm the Florida LLC is active in the official Sunbiz search.
  2. Define what activity or assets the series is intended to hold.
  3. Review the current official instructions and fee before submitting.
  4. Save confirmations and internal records.

No general rule requires every Florida LLC to use a protected series. The structure can raise legal, tax, accounting and insurance questions that deserve professional review.
