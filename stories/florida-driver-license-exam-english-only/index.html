Florida Driver License Exams Are Now English-Only: What Changed and How to Prepare

The tests are no longer offered in Spanish. If you learned traffic rules in another language, prepare for the questions and instructions in English.

By Trámites123 · Published September 26, 2026 · Official sources listed below

FLHSMV announced that beginning February 6, 2026, knowledge and driving-skills exams for all driver-license classes are administered in English. The policy includes oral exams. According to the state announcement, translation services are not permitted during those tests and printed exams in other languages were removed.

The key point

You can study traffic rules in Spanish, but you should be ready to understand the exam questions and driving instructions in English.

Who is affected?

People who must take a knowledge or driving-skills test to obtain a Florida driver license, including oral testing. The policy concerns the language used to administer the tests; it is not a general order requiring everyone with a valid license to retake an exam.

How to prepare

  1. Learn the traffic rule and the English term together.
  2. Use the current official driver handbook.
  3. Practice questions in English without memorizing isolated answers.
  4. Practice common driving instructions such as turn left, turn right, stop, back up and park.
  5. Confirm your appointment, office and required documents through an official source.

Official sources

  • FLHSMV: English-only exam announcement.
  • FLHSMV: Class E tests.
  • FLHSMV: driver handbooks.
