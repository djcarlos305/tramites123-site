Your Florida Driver License Number May Change When You Renew

A renewed or replacement credential may carry a new number. Here is what to check afterward.

By Trámites123 · Published September 27, 2026 · Official sources listed below

FLHSMV announced that beginning July 31, 2024, newly issued, renewed or replaced Florida driver licenses and ID cards receive a number containing at least four randomized digits.

The key point

The change happens when a new, renewed or replacement credential is issued. It does not mean every older credential became invalid on that date.

Why it matters for paperwork

If an old number is saved in a profile or template, a later transaction may fail. Use the number on your current credential when a form asks for it.

After receiving the credential

  1. Review name, address, class, restrictions and expiration date.
  2. Update active records where the organization actually requires the current credential.
  3. Protect saved license information as carefully as the physical card.
  4. Avoid sending a full license number or photo through unsolicited links.

The numbering change is separate from Florida's later policy on driver-license exam language.

Official source
