Can't Pay Your Miami-Dade Water Bill? Here Are Your Options

Start with the account balance and deadlines, then check whether an extension, arrangement or high-bill review applies.

By Trámites123 · Published September 27, 2026 · Official sources listed below

If your service is billed by Miami-Dade Water and Sewer Department (WASD), start by checking the balance, due date and any final notice. The county offers online account options for payment extensions and describes arrangements for past-due balances.

Act before a final notice

Check your current account and notices rather than relying on an old example. If you need more time, look for the payment-extension option in the official account portal.

If you owe several billing cycles

WASD's past-due balance information describes payment arrangements in which customers continue paying the current bill plus the arrangement amount. Confirm the exact terms for your account.

If the bill looks unusually high

Compare usage with previous bills and check for visible leaks. WASD allows customers to request a high-bill investigation online or by calling 305-665-7477. An investigation does not guarantee a credit.
