Compress PDF

Reduce a PDF file size for email or online uploads.

Lower quality creates a smaller file. Text is converted into an image.

🔒 The PDF is processed on your device.

How to compress a PDF

Select the file, choose a quality level and download the compressed copy. Review the result before deleting the original.

Practical example

A 12 MB scanned PDF may become small enough for email, although the result depends on page count and image resolution.

Important limitations

The tool rebuilds pages as images. Selectable text, links and interactive forms may be lost. Protected PDFs may not open.

Frequently asked questions

Is my PDF uploaded to the server?

No. Processing happens locally in your browser.

Why do some files barely shrink?

PDFs that are already optimized or mainly contain text may have little room for additional compression.

Will the quality remain identical?

Compression reduces visual quality to save space. Choose a higher setting when the document contains small text.
