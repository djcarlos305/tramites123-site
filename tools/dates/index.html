Practical guide

Organize dates, deadlines and important periods

Use these tools to count days, calculate age, review holidays, estimate school dates or check time worked. They help with planning and do not replace an institution's official rules.

Check the official date

A portal, school, employer or agency can use its own rules and hours. Always confirm the deadline shown on your application or notice.

Use results carefully

  1. Enter complete dates and verify the year.
  2. Distinguish calendar days from business days when a requirement says so.
  3. Keep the official confirmation for an appointment, filing or request.

Find the right tool

Use Date Counter for periods between two dates, Holiday Calendar for planning, and Age, Dates, Work and PTO for personal calculations.

Frequently asked questions

Do holidays change an official deadline?

It depends on the agency, school, company or court. Use the calendar to plan, then confirm that organization's official rule.

Can I use the date counter for an application deadline?

Yes, as an estimate. Check whether the deadline uses calendar or business days and confirm the final date with the organization receiving the application.
