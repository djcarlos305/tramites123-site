Frequently asked questions

Does savings count as an expense?

This planner treats savings as money assigned to your goals, so it reduces the amount available for spending.

What should I include under other expenses?

Add childcare, subscriptions, pets, education, family support or any expense that is not listed above.

Are my income and expenses stored?

No. Calculations happen in your browser and are not stored on our server.
