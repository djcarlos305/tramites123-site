Real-life use

When would I use this tool?

You need to sign a letter or digital document, but do not have a printer or scanner. This tool creates a PNG image of your signature for a document you control.

Good to know

A signature image alone does not replace requirements for an electronic, notarized or agency-specific signature.

How to use it

  1. Draw your signature in the box.
  2. Clear it and try again if you do not like the result.
  3. Download the PNG and save it in a private place.

Is the signature saved by the website?

No. The image is created in your browser and downloaded to your device.

Can I use it on any document?

Use it only when the recipient accepts an image signature. Confirm special requirements before sending.
