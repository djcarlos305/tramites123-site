When would I use this tool?

You found a car you like and the dealer quoted a monthly payment. This calculator helps you see the full number before agreeing: price, down payment, trade-in, tax, interest and term.

Good to know

This is an estimate. It does not include every dealer fee, insurance, registration cost or credit approval.

How to use it

  1. Enter the vehicle price and down payment.
  2. Add trade-in value, tax and interest rate when known.
  3. Compare different terms before deciding.
