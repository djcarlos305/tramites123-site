Practical guide

Simple tools for administrative work

This collection brings together utilities for preparing documents, organizing information and creating basic materials for a small business. They work well for drafts and everyday tasks.

Review your information

Before downloading or sending a document, verify names, dates, amounts, taxes and contact details.

A simple workflow

  1. Prepare a list of the documents you need.
  2. Create a letter, invoice, receipt, signature or QR code for the task.
  3. Save a copy and review the final result for completeness.

Not professional advice

These utilities do not replace legal, tax, accounting or permit requirements. Use them for organization and confirm obligations with the appropriate source.
