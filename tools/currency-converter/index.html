How to use the converter

Enter an amount, select the starting and destination currencies, and press Convert. Use Swap currencies to reverse the conversion quickly.

Practical example

If you convert 100 U.S. dollars to Colombian pesos, the tool multiplies 100 by the latest informational rate available.

Important limitations

Rates move throughout the day and may differ from banks, cards, exchange offices and informal markets. Confirm the final amount before sending money or making a purchase.

Frequently asked questions

How often do rates change?

The provider may update rates several times a day, but the displayed number is not necessarily a real-time quote.

Does the conversion include fees?

No. Banks, cards and transfer services may add fees or use a different exchange rate.

Can I use the result for a transaction?

Use it as a reference and confirm the final amount with the institution processing the payment.
