How to use the calendar

Choose a year and month, filter by type or search by name. Use the previous and next buttons to navigate, or print the monthly calendar.

Practical example

To plan a November appointment, select that month and the Federal category to see federal holidays displayed in the calendar.

Important limitations

Fun days are unofficial observances. Religious and cultural dates can vary, and each school, business or agency sets its own operating hours.

Frequently asked questions

Do businesses close for every federal holiday?

No. Closures depend on each organization. Confirm directly before visiting.

Are fun days official holidays?

No. They are identified as unofficial observances.

Can I print the entire year?

Yes. Select All months and use the print button. Each month is prepared as a separate page.
