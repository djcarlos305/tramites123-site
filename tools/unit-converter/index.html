Real-life use

When would I use this tool?

A recipe, package, room or form uses a different measurement than the one you have. This converter avoids quick mental math mistakes between inches, centimeters, pounds, kilos and more.

Good to know

The result is mathematical. If a form requires an official measurement, use the source or instrument named by that institution.

How to use it

  1. Choose a measurement category.
  2. Enter the amount and choose the two units.
  3. Copy or note the result at the precision you need.
