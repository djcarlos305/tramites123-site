Practical guide

Use money calculators as a starting point

These calculators help organize everyday numbers: payments, budgets, percentages, salary, loans and housing. They provide educational estimates for comparing scenarios before a decision.

Important

Results are not a credit offer, quote, or financial, tax, legal or mortgage advice.

Get a useful estimate

  1. Use current numbers for rates, down payment, monthly costs and debt.
  2. Try two or three scenarios to see what changes.
  3. Confirm final figures with your lender, employer, insurer or qualified professional.

Pick the right calculator

Start with a budget to organize expenses, a loan or mortgage calculator for estimated payments, and percentages for everyday discounts and tax.

Frequently asked questions

Are currency-converter rates a final quote?

No. Rates are informational and can vary by provider, time, fee and payment method.

Does the mortgage calculator include every purchase cost?

It includes the values you enter, such as tax, insurance, HOA and PMI. It does not replace a loan quote or include every closing cost.
