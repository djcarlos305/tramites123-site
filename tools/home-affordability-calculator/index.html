Real-life use

When would I use this tool?

You want to know whether a home is within your budget before touring properties or talking to a lender. This tool turns income, debt and estimated costs into a starting number.

Good to know

An affordability estimate is not a preapproval and does not guarantee a lender will offer that amount.

How to use it

  1. Enter income, monthly debts and down payment.
  2. Include a realistic interest rate and monthly costs.
  3. Try a conservative number and a higher scenario to compare.

Frequently asked questions

Which debts should I include?

Include monthly payments that affect your budget, such as auto, card, loan or support payments.

Does the number include insurance and taxes?

It includes only what you enter. Confirm real tax, insurance, HOA and PMI costs with qualified sources.
