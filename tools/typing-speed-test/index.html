What does this test measure?

WPM means words per minute. Gross speed uses typed characters divided by five. Adjusted speed discounts errors to provide a more useful measure of speed with accuracy.

How to improve

Start with one minute at medium difficulty. Prioritize accuracy before speed. Once you can maintain 95% accuracy or better, try advanced passages or numbers and punctuation.

Available challenges

Use General for everyday practice, Office / admin for administrative text, and Numbers & punctuation for dates, amounts, symbols and common formats.
