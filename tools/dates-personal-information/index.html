Real-life use

When would I use this tool?

Sometimes you need an exact age, time worked, days between dates or an estimated PTO balance without doing several calculations separately. This page brings those personal calculations together.

Good to know

Calculations run in the browser. Do not enter Social Security, banking, medical or case-number information.

How to use it

  1. Choose the calculation you need.
  2. Enter complete dates and hours without sharing sensitive numbers.
  3. Review the result and save it only when needed.

Frequently asked questions

Are my dates or work details saved?

No. Calculations run on this device and do not require an account.

Does the PTO calculation replace my employer's policy?

No. Confirm accrual, limits and time-off approval with your employer.
