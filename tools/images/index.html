Practical guide

Prepare images for forms, email and documents

A photo can look fine on a phone and still be too large, in an unsupported format or the wrong dimensions for a portal. This collection handles those common problems.

Before you begin

Keep the original photo and work from a copy so you can compare quality, size and crop.

Choose by the problem

  1. Use the image compressor when the file is too large.
  2. Convert HEIC to JPG if a website does not accept iPhone photos.
  3. Prepare a 2×2 photo when you need a square print format.

For applications and uploads

First convert the image to an accepted format, then adjust the maximum size. If you need to send it with paperwork, create a PDF from the finished images.

Compress to a limit · HEIC to JPG · Images to PDF

Frequently asked questions

Does the tool change my original photo?

No. Your original photo stays on your device. You download a new copy with the changes.

What if a portal requires an image under 1 MB?

Open Compress Image to a Maximum Size, choose 1 MB or enter the limit given by the portal, then download the result.
