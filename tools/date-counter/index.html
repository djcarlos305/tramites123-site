Real-life use

When would I use this tool?

You have an appointment, trip or deadline and do not want to count a calendar by hand. This counter shows the days between two dates so you can plan more clearly.

Good to know

Some rules use business days, office hours or special deadlines. Confirm requirements with the relevant organization.

How to use it

  1. Choose a start date and an end date.
  2. Check the year and month before calculating.
  3. Use the result as a reference and keep the official date for your appointment or application.
