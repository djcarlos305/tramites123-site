Made for upload limits

Enter the maximum accepted by a portal. The tool adjusts quality and dimensions until the file fits below that limit.

JPG output

Images download as JPG files. Transparent areas in PNG images are converted to a white background.

Real-life use

When would I use this tool?

A portal says “maximum 1 MB” and your photo is much larger. This tool exists to make a smaller copy you can upload without retaking the picture.

Good to know

Compression can reduce detail. Keep the original photo and never use a compressed copy as your only backup.

How to use it

  1. Choose the image and enter the limit required by the portal.
  2. Choose a quality setting if the tool asks for one.
  3. Download the copy and check that it is clear before uploading.

Frequently asked questions

Is the original image deleted?

No. The tool creates a new copy for download.

Can this guarantee a portal will accept my photo?

No. The tool reduces file size; a portal may have additional format or dimension rules.
