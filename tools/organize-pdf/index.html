Real-life use

When would I use this tool?

Your PDF has pages in the wrong order, a duplicate sheet or a page turned sideways. This tool lets you prepare a cleaner copy before sharing it.

Good to know

The tool does not change the original file. Keep a copy of important documents and check that you do not remove needed information.

How to use it

  1. Choose the PDF and review its pages.
  2. Reorder, rotate or remove only the pages you need.
  3. Download the copy and open it to confirm the final order.
