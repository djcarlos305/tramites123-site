When would I use this tool?

You are comparing a personal loan, financed purchase or consolidation and want to see the monthly payment before committing. This calculator separates estimated payment and total interest.

Good to know

This is an educational estimate. It does not include every fee, insurance cost or condition of a real offer.

How to use it

  1. Enter the amount you may borrow.
  2. Enter the rate and term you were offered or want to compare.
  3. Try more than one option to see the effect on payment and interest.

Frequently asked questions

Is the rate I enter APR or interest?

Use the figure provided by the lender and check whether it includes fees. The calculator does not replace offer documents.

Is a lower monthly payment always better?

Not necessarily. A longer term can lower the payment and increase total interest.
