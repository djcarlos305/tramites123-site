Real-life use

When would I use this tool?

You took a photo of a document, but it came out dark, tilted or hard to read. This tool helps create a clearer copy to review, store or later convert to PDF.

Good to know

Improving a photo does not correct information or guarantee an agency or portal will accept it. Do not upload unnecessary sensitive data.

How to use it

  1. Choose a readable document photo.
  2. Adjust brightness, contrast or rotation as needed.
  3. Download the copy and verify every important detail is still visible.

Frequently asked questions

Is my original file changed?

No. The tool creates a new image copy.

Can it repair a completely blurry photo?

Not always. It can improve contrast and orientation, but cannot recover details missing from the original image.
