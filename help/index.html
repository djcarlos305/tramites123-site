RESOURCES FOR OUR COMMUNITY

Need food or help paying bills?

You are not alone. Start with a call or find a resource near you. These direct links serve Miami-Dade, Broward, Palm Beach, Monroe, and the rest of Florida.

Reviewed September 27, 2026

If you need food today

Call 211, give your ZIP code, and say: “I need a food pantry or meal available today.” You can also search Feeding South Florida. Before traveling, confirm the hours, address, access, and any requirements. Tell 211 if you cannot get to a distribution site.

Food today or this week

Call 211 ↗

Give your ZIP code and ask for a nearby food pantry, prepared meals, rent help, or utility assistance. This is a good first step anywhere in Florida.

Feeding South Florida: find a pantry ↗

A network of pantries and meal programs in Miami-Dade, Broward, Palm Beach, and Monroe. Search for a partner near you.

Feeding South Florida: distribution calendar ↗

Additional food distributions. Check the schedule on the day you plan to go; times and locations can change.

Feeding South Florida: program directory ↗

Search by location and type of help. For guidance, call 954-518-1857 in Miami-Dade and Broward or 561-659-5070 in Palm Beach.

Feeding Florida: find a food bank ↗

Find the food bank network for your county if you live elsewhere in Florida.

Groceries and family benefits

MyACCESS: SNAP and temporary cash assistance ↗

Florida’s official portal to apply for SNAP, Temporary Cash Assistance (TCA), and other benefits. The agency determines eligibility.

Florida DCF: how to apply ↗

Application options, documents, and follow-up. A SNAP or TCA interview may be required.

Florida WIC ↗

Nutrition support for eligible pregnant and postpartum people, infants, and young children. Contact your county health department for an appointment.

Summer meals for children ↗

The Florida Department of Health explains meal programs when school is out. Check each season’s locations and dates.

Meals for older adults in Miami-Dade ↗

The county’s community services include home-delivered and senior-center meals for eligible older adults.

Miami-Dade and Monroe

211 Miami ↗

Confidential help 24/7 in English, Spanish, and Creole for Miami-Dade and Monroe. You can also text your ZIP code to 898211.

Miami-Dade: community food assistance ↗

Local guidance for food and other needs.

Miami-Dade: community services ↗

County entry point for food, rent, utility, and other support. Availability depends on each program.

Miami-Dade: help with electric bills ↗

Check eligibility and available funding for electric-bill assistance.

Miami-Dade: water-bill options ↗

A Spanish-language guide to extensions, payment arrangements, and unusually high bill investigations.

Broward

211 Broward ↗

A 24-hour helpline for food, housing, utilities, health, and other needs.

Feeding South Florida in Broward ↗

Find a partner pantry. Call before visiting to confirm hours and requirements.

Connect with local food, housing, health, and other services.

Palm Beach: utility assistance ↗

County housing, utility, employment, and other support.

Find the 211 center serving another Florida county.

Before sharing personal information

Hours, funding, and eligibility rules change. Confirm details with each organization before visiting or applying. Share personal details only through an official site or phone number. Trámites123 does not decide eligibility, process benefits, or charge for this list.
